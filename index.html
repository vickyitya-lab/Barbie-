Before you book, read this

Our Policies

  • 01 Please book at least 24 hours ahead of time. This helps us make sure your spot is ready and waiting for you.
  • 02 We ask for a 30% deposit to lock in your appointment. Just a heads up, if you cancel less than 24 hours before, that deposit is non-refundable. We totally understand things come up, but our time matters too!
  • 03 Need to move your appointment? No problem at all, just give us at least 24 hours' notice and your deposit will be safe.
  • 04 Arrive on time if you're more than 30 minutes late, your appointment may need to be rescheduled.
  • 05 Come with your hair clean and detangled please! It helps us give you the best results and keeps your appointment running on time.
  • 06 We only use high-quality products that keep your hair healthy, moisturized, and looking shiny and fresh.
  • 07 If this is your first time booking with us, you get a special discount just for trusting us with your hair. Welcome to the family!
  • 08 Tell a friend about us! When they book, you both walk away with a discount on your next visit. The more the merrier.
  • 09 We keep it flexible! You can pay with Cash, Cash App, Apple Pay, or Zelle. Whatever works best for you.
  • 10 Got any allergies or scalp sensitivities? Please let us know before your appointment. Your comfort and safety always come first.
